- The bridge under construction during the 1995 Great Hanshin earthquake was the Akashi Kaikyō Bridge.
- The Akashi Kaikyō Bridge became the longest-span suspension bridge in the world when it opened.
- The main span of the Akashi Kaikyō Bridge is 1,991 meters.
- Prior to the completion of the Akashi Kaikyō Bridge in 1998, the longest suspension span was held by the Humber Bridge.
- The Humber Bridge is located in England.
- The main span of the Humber Bridge is 1,410 meters.
