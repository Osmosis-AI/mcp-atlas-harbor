Antoni Gaudí's workshop located at the Sagrada Família was looted during the Spanish Civil War in 1936.
The Sagrada Família is located at Carrer de Mallorca, 401, L'Eixample, 08013 Barcelona, Spain (latitude 41.4036299, longitude 2.1743558).
The Sagrada Família has an elevation of 32.24 meters.
Casa Batlló was originally built in 1875 by Emili Sala Cortés and was later renovated by Antoni Gaudí.
Casa Batlló is located at Pg. de Gràcia, 43, L'Eixample, 08007 Barcelona, Spain (latitude 41.3917, longitude 2.164918).
Casa Batlló has an elevation of 28.28 meters.
