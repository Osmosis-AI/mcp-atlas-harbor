The famous earthquake that triggered a tsunami in Japan in 2011 was the Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami.
The Tōhoku earthquake and tsunami resulted in 19,759 deaths in Japan.
The cancelled study on asundexian and apixaban that was sponsored by Bayer and conducted in Tohoku is title "A Multicenter, International, Randomized, Active Comparator-controlled, Double-blind, Double-dummy, Parallel-group, 2-arm, Phase 3 Study to Compare the Efficacy and Safety of the Oral FXIa Inhibitor Asundexian (BAY 2433334) With Apixaban for the Prevention of Stroke or Systemic Embolism in Male and Female Participants Aged 18 Years and Older With Atrial Fibrillation at Risk for Stroke
The study "A Multicenter, International, Randomized, Active Comparator-controlled, Double-blind, Double-dummy, Parallel-group, 2-arm, Phase 3 Study to Compare the Efficacy and Safety of the Oral FXIa Inhibitor Asundexian (BAY 2433334) With Apixaban for the Prevention of Stroke or Systemic Embolism in Male and Female Participants Aged 18 Years and Older With Atrial Fibrillation at Risk for Stroke", was cancelled based on a recommendation from the Independent Data Monitoring Committee (IDMC).
