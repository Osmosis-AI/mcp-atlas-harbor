The last commit in the snake-game repository introduced the game's README file
Both the artworks titled Bird and Horse depict animals and thus fit the theme of animals in art
The artwork Bird is by Suzuki Osamu and is displayed in the Asian Art department of the Metropolitan Museum of Art
The dimensions of Bird are H. 4 5/8 in. (11.7 cm), W. 6 1/4 in. (15.9 cm), and D. 4 3/8 in. (11.1 cm)
The artwork Horse is by Suzuki Osamu and is displayed in the Asian Art department of the Metropolitan Museum of Art
The dimensions of Horse are H. 15 in. (38.1 cm), W. 8 in. (20.3 cm), and D. 4 1/2 in. (11.4 cm)
