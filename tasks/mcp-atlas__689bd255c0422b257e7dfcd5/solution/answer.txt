The place where, in the 1900s, paleontologists unearthed the Age of Mammals when they found A skeletons of extinct Miocene mammals in the hills of Nebraska is "Agate Fossil Beds National Monument
The "Agate Fossil Beds National Monument" full address is 301 River Road, Harrison, Nebraska, United States.
The "Agate Fossil Beds National Monument" is open from dawn till dusk every day.
The "Agate Fossil Beds National Monument" the total area is 3,057.87 acres (12.3748 km2).
